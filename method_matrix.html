Methodology gaps to keep explicit

  • Most leaderboards cannot support pure model or harness attribution without crossed designs.
  • Trace artifacts need a parsed event schema before trajectory claims.
  • Missingness conventions need standardized sensitivity reporting.
  • Small slices need powered thresholds or shrinkage before strong slice claims.
  • Non-separation is not equivalence; equivalence needs a margin and test.
  • Temporal drift needs repeated benchmark snapshots.
  • Judge or reward reliability and severity labels are inconsistent or absent.

Benchmark TLDR

Report alongside rank Uncertainty, adjacent-rank resolution, non-separated rank bands, rank-stability intervals, top-K probabilities, and MDE scale.
Name the compared object Submission, config, model, model plus harness, or full agent system. Do not silently upgrade one into another.
Show data support Task coverage, missing cells, repeated-run counts, slice sizes, and whether repeated rows are independent evidence.
Keep caveats attached Non-significance is not equivalence. More runs on the same small task set may not solve task-composition uncertainty.
